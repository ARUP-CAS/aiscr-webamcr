## Report a vulnerability

In the repository's **Security and quality** tab, open **Advisories** and choose **Report a vulnerability** to send a private report to the maintainers.

If private reporting is unavailable, use an established private maintainer contact.
If you need a contact method, open an issue asking only how to reach the maintainers privately.
Keep the reason for the request, the report, and its evidence in private channels.
